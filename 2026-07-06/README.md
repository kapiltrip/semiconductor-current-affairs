## Technical Terms / Deep Definitions

Term: American Depositary Share (ADS) and American Depositary Receipt (ADR)
Definition: An ADS is the U.S.-traded ownership interest representing shares of a non-U.S. company held through a U.S. depositary bank; an ADR is the negotiable certificate evidencing that ADS interest. Market participants often use ADS and ADR interchangeably. The structure solves currency, settlement, custody, and market-access friction by letting U.S. investors trade in dollars through U.S. systems. For SK hynix, ten U.S. depositary shares represent one Korean common share, so each U.S. unit corresponds to one-tenth of a Korean share. Source: [SEC Investor Bulletin](https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/general-resources/news-alerts/alerts-bulletins/investor-bulletins-88)

Term: Depositary bank
Definition: A depositary bank holds or arranges custody of the foreign company's underlying shares, issues the U.S. depositary securities, maintains holder records, processes dividends and voting instructions, and may charge custody or service fees. It solves the operational problem of connecting two securities markets and legal systems. Citibank filed SK hynix's Form F-6, showing that the bank is the depositary responsible for the receipt programme; it does not manufacture chips or decide memory capacity. Source: [SEC Form F-6 filing index](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2120882/000119380526000898/0001193805-26-000898-index.htm)

Term: Depositary ratio
Definition: A depositary ratio states how many U.S. depositary shares represent one underlying foreign share, or vice versa. It solves practical pricing by making the U.S. unit trade at a familiar dollar amount even when the home-market share is very expensive. Reuters reports ten SK hynix depositary shares per Korean common share. Therefore, 17.79 million newly issued common shares could support 177.9 million depositary shares if the full amount is sold; that is arithmetic from the reported ratio, not the final allocated amount. Source: [Reuters via MarketScreener](https://www.marketscreener.com/news/south-korea-s-sk-hynix-launching-28-billion-us-listing-to-ride-global-ai-wave-ce7f5edadf88f327)

Term: Primary offering
Definition: A primary offering sells newly issued company shares so the issuer receives capital. It solves a funding need for factories, equipment, research, acquisitions, or debt reduction. It differs from a secondary sale in which an existing shareholder sells already outstanding shares and receives the proceeds. SK hynix plans to issue new common shares behind the U.S. depositary offering, so the transaction can fund manufacturing investment but also increases the share count. Source: [SK hynix Form F-1 filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2120882/000119312526280172/d32785df1.htm)

Term: Shareholder dilution
Definition: Dilution is the reduction in an existing shareholder's proportional ownership and potentially earnings per share when a company issues additional shares. It solves no manufacturing problem by itself; it is the cost side of raising equity capital without repayment obligations. If the new cash generates returns above its cost, the company can become more valuable even though each old share owns a smaller percentage. SK hynix investors are weighing that long-term capacity benefit against immediate new-share supply, helping explain why the Korean stock could fall even as the company raises growth capital. Source: [Investor.gov dilution explanation](https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/investing-basics/glossary/convertible-securities)

Term: Bookbuilding
Definition: Bookbuilding is the process in which underwriters collect indications of demand from prospective investors across prices and quantities, then use that order book to recommend the final offering price and allocation. It solves price discovery for a large new issue before public trading begins. Reuters says SK hynix management is meeting global investors this week, with final pricing expected Thursday and trading expected Friday. The roughly US$28 billion figure is therefore an estimate linked to the Seoul share price, not guaranteed proceeds. Source: [Reuters via MarketScreener](https://www.marketscreener.com/news/south-korea-s-sk-hynix-launching-28-billion-us-listing-to-ride-global-ai-wave-ce7f5edadf88f327)

Term: Form F-1
Definition: Form F-1 is the SEC registration statement commonly used by a foreign private issuer to offer securities publicly in the United States. It solves investor-disclosure requirements by providing extensive business, financial, risk, ownership, offering, and management information. SK hynix filed its F-1 on June 24. The filing establishes a proposed offering framework, but it does not guarantee successful pricing, final proceeds, or the trading date. Source: [SEC filing index](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2120882/000119312526280172/0001193125-26-280172-index.htm)

Term: Form F-6
Definition: Form F-6 registers the depositary securities and their deposit agreement. It solves the legal and contractual setup for the U.S. receipt programme, including the depositary bank, fees, and rights attached to the receipts. It contains less operating-company information than the capital-raising F-1. SK hynix's July 1 F-6 was filed by Citibank, while the F-1 carries the issuer and offering disclosure. Source: [SEC Investor Bulletin](https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/general-resources/news-alerts/alerts-bulletins/investor-bulletins-88)

Term: Capital expenditure (capex)
Definition: Capex is cash spent on long-lived productive assets such as land, buildings, cleanrooms, lithography systems, deposition and etch tools, packaging lines, and test equipment. It solves future capacity or technology needs, but spending is not current output. SK hynix says offering proceeds will support Korean fabs and equipment. The conversion chain is cash to construction, installed tools, process qualification, yield ramp, customer qualification, and only then shipped memory. Source: [Reuters via MarketScreener](https://www.marketscreener.com/news/south-korea-s-sk-hynix-launching-28-billion-us-listing-to-ride-global-ai-wave-ce7f5edadf88f327)

Term: Extreme ultraviolet (EUV) lithography
Definition: EUV lithography patterns a wafer using 13.5 nm light and reflective optics in vacuum. It solves the difficulty of printing the most intricate layers of advanced logic and DRAM with fewer multi-patterning steps than older deep-ultraviolet flows. EUV scanners are extremely complex and capital intensive, so an equity raise can finance only part of a broader fab, process, metrology, materials, and workforce system. SK hynix identified ASML EUV scanners among intended equipment purchases. Source: [ASML](https://www.asml.com/en/en/products/euv-lithography-systems)

Term: High-Bandwidth Memory (HBM)
Definition: HBM stacks multiple DRAM dies and connects them through dense vertical and package-level interconnects to provide a very wide, energy-efficient data path near an AI accelerator. It solves the memory-bandwidth bottleneck that leaves compute units idle while waiting for data. HBM is not just ordinary RAM at a higher clock: it requires advanced DRAM processes, thin-die handling, stacking, bonding, thermal control, known-good-die screening, and package integration. SK hynix's financing matters because both front-end wafer capacity and back-end HBM packaging must scale together. Source: [SK hynix HBM technical explainer](https://news.skhynix.com/become-a-semiconductor-expert-with-sk-hynix-hbm/)

Term: Operating profit
Definition: Operating profit is revenue minus costs and expenses from core operations before financing costs and income taxes. It solves the question of how profitable the underlying business was without mixing in capital structure or tax effects. Analysts forecast Samsung's second-quarter operating profit, but Samsung had not reported the result at today's research cutoff. A high operating profit can reflect higher memory prices and volume, while provisions, bonuses, depreciation, and product mix can change the final figure. Source: [Reuters via MarketScreener](https://www.marketscreener.com/news/samsung-likely-to-post-18-fold-jump-in-profit-on-surging-ai-demand-for-memory-ce7f5edadf88f722)

Term: Analyst consensus
Definition: Analyst consensus aggregates independent forecasts into a market expectation against which the reported result is compared. It solves the need for a common benchmark, not the need for audited facts. Reuters cites an LSEG SmartEstimate weighted toward analysts with stronger track records. Samsung can report a record profit and still disappoint the market if the number is below the expectation already embedded in prices. Source: [Reuters via MarketScreener](https://www.marketscreener.com/news/samsung-likely-to-post-18-fold-jump-in-profit-on-surging-ai-demand-for-memory-ce7f5edadf88f722)

Term: Average selling price (ASP)
Definition: ASP is product revenue divided by units or an industry-specific quantity such as bits shipped. It solves the pricing question when a company sells many grades, capacities, and contract terms. A rising memory ASP can increase revenue and margins even if physical unit growth is modest. Citi estimates quoted by Reuters put second-quarter DRAM and NAND ASP increases at 44% and 53% quarter over quarter; these are analyst estimates, not yet Samsung-reported figures. Source: [Reuters via MarketScreener](https://www.marketscreener.com/news/samsung-likely-to-post-18-fold-jump-in-profit-on-surging-ai-demand-for-memory-ce7f5edadf88f722)

Term: Earnings quiet period
Definition: An earnings quiet period is a company-defined interval before results when management limits contact with investors and avoids selective discussion of material performance information. It solves fair-disclosure and information-asymmetry risk. TSMC states that its quiet period runs from July 6 through July 15 before the July 16 earnings conference. Silence during this interval is not negative evidence and should not be filled with rumor. Source: [TSMC Investor Relations](https://investor.tsmc.com/english/quarterly-results/2026/q2)

Term: Financial guidance
Definition: Guidance is management's forward-looking range for revenue, margins, spending, or other business metrics based on current assumptions. It solves investor planning but remains uncertain until results are reported. TSMC's official second-quarter guidance is US$39.0-40.2 billion of revenue, 65.5%-67.5% gross margin, and 56.5%-58.5% operating margin. Citi's expectation that TSMC may raise longer-term growth guidance is an analyst view, not updated company guidance. Source: [TSMC Investor Relations](https://investor.tsmc.com/english/quarterly-results/2026/q2)

Term: Long-term purchase agreement (LTA)
Definition: An LTA commits a buyer and supplier to quantities, capacity reservations, pricing formulas, deposits, or purchase obligations over an extended period. It solves planning uncertainty in a capital-intensive market by giving the supplier demand visibility and the buyer access security. The tradeoff is reduced flexibility if prices fall or demand changes. Reported SEMI policy arguments favor capacity investment and LTAs over government-set pricing or allocation. Source: [Tom's Hardware report on the SEMI letter](https://www.tomshardware.com/tech-industry/sk-hynix-samsung-micron-among-semiconductor-industry-group-lobbying-against-government-intervention-on-domestic-memory-chip-supply-says-move-would-worsen-situation-suggests-tax-deductions-on-consumer-electronics-instead)

Term: Supply allocation
Definition: Supply allocation is the rule used to divide scarce output among customers, products, countries, or end uses. It solves an immediate shortage but creates opportunity costs because supplying one segment reduces availability elsewhere. A political priority for automotive chips may protect vehicle production while delaying servers, consumer devices, networking, or medical equipment. The U.S. debate matters because memory capacity cannot be redirected instantly across HBM, conventional DRAM, NAND, package types, qualifications, and customer contracts. Sources: [Industry coalition letter](https://tiaonline.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Industry-Coalition-Letter-re-Memory-Shortage.pdf) and [Senator Moreno release](https://www.moreno.senate.gov/press-releases/moreno-calls-to-restrict-sale-of-memory-chips-abroad-until-us-auto-industry-fully-supplied/)

## 1. SK hynix: Capital-Market Access Becomes Manufacturing Finance

### Confirmed facts

SK hynix filed a Form F-1 on June 24 and a Citibank-filed Form F-6 on July 1. Reuters reports that the U.S. offering process launches July 6, covering up to 17.79 million newly issued Korean common shares represented at a ratio of ten depositary shares per common share. The final price is expected Thursday, with Nasdaq trading expected Friday under the proposed programme.

Reuters estimates roughly US$28 billion of proceeds from the Korean share price. This is not a fixed transaction value. Investor demand, final price, allocations, underwriting deductions, foreign exchange, market movement, and the number actually sold determine net proceeds.

### Why this is not merely a dual-listing story

If SK hynix only made existing Korean shares easier to trade in New York, market access would be the main story. Here, new shares are being issued. That creates two simultaneous effects:

```text
new shares -> more cash for SK hynix -> future fabs and tools
new shares -> larger share count -> lower proportional ownership for old shares
```

The Korean stock was down 4% during Monday trading even though it remained about 273% higher for the year. The decline does not prove investors oppose the strategy; it reflects a mixture of dilution, offering supply, valuation, and concern about the maturity of the memory cycle.

### Capital does not become HBM immediately

Reuters says proceeds will support Korean fabs and equipment including ASML EUV scanners. The physical conversion chain is long:

```text
offering proceeds -> land/building/utilities -> tool orders and delivery
-> installation -> process qualification -> yield ramp
-> HBM stack/package qualification -> customer volume
```

EUV improves advanced DRAM patterning, but an EUV scanner alone cannot produce HBM. Deposition, etch, clean, metrology, wafer test, thinning, vertical interconnect, stacking, molding, thermal management, final test, and customer qualification must scale together.

### Confirmed versus analysis

Confirmed: SEC registrations, proposed U.S. programme, reported new-share amount and ratio, Monday launch, intended manufacturing use, and expected pricing/trading sequence. Pending: final price, shares sold, net proceeds, investor allocation, effective listing, index inclusion, capex schedule, tool deliveries, qualified output, and return on invested capital.

Analysis: U.S. access can broaden ownership and reduce trading friction, but a higher valuation is not automatic. The long-run result depends on whether new capacity earns attractive returns after depreciation and whether AI-memory demand remains durable when that capacity reaches production.

### India and career relevance

India's fab and OSAT projects face the same capital-to-output discipline. Large financing announcements should be tracked through equipment, process, qualification, yield, and shipments. Relevant careers include semiconductor finance, investor relations, fab planning, equipment engineering, process integration, HBM packaging, test, yield analytics, supply-chain planning, and technology-market research.

Simple explanation: SK hynix is selling new U.S.-traded ownership units to raise factory money. Investors get easier access; current owners accept some dilution; customers get more memory only after years of construction and qualification.

## 2. Samsung: An 18-Fold Profit Increase Is Still A Forecast

Reuters reports an LSEG SmartEstimate of 86 trillion won for Samsung's second-quarter operating profit, about eighteen times the 4.7 trillion won reported a year earlier. Samsung is expected to release its preliminary estimate on July 7 and detailed earnings later in the month.

### What drives the forecast

Analysts point to scarce memory supply, AI inference growth, and rising prices across HBM, conventional DRAM, and NAND. Citi estimates cited by Reuters put second-quarter DRAM and NAND ASP increases at 44% and 53% quarter over quarter.

This is a pricing-and-mix mechanism:

```text
tight supply + stronger demand -> higher ASP
-> more revenue per bit -> operating leverage -> higher profit
```

But final operating profit also depends on shipment volume, product mix, yields, depreciation, employee bonus provisions, inventory accounting, and non-memory businesses.

### Confirmed versus pending

Confirmed: the analyst estimate exists and is market-moving. Not confirmed: Samsung's actual revenue, operating profit, divisional result, ASP, HBM qualification volume, or outlook. The note will treat the forecast as **open** until the company reports.

### Why the market can fall on strong earnings

Share prices compare results with expectations, not with zero. A record result can disappoint if investors expected more, if margins peak, or if future capex suggests oversupply. Conversely, a lower result can be welcomed if guidance improves. This is why students must label `forecast`, `guidance`, and `actual` separately.

India relevance: expensive memory can raise costs for phones, PCs, servers, vehicles, and electronics assembled in India. It also strengthens the case for memory-controller, validation, firmware, packaging, test, and supply-chain skills even before domestic DRAM fabrication exists.

Simple explanation: analysts think Samsung made far more money because memory became scarce and expensive. The company has not yet confirmed the number.

## 3. TSMC: Quiet Period Starts, Expectations Rise

TSMC's investor page states that its quiet period runs from July 6 through July 15 and that its second-quarter conference is scheduled for July 16. Official guidance is US$39.0-40.2 billion of revenue, 65.5%-67.5% gross margin, and 56.5%-58.5% operating margin.

Reuters-linked reporting says Citi raised its target price and expects TSMC may increase its 2026 and longer-term growth outlook, citing demand across AI GPUs, custom accelerators, cloud processors, networking, optics, CPUs, advanced nodes, and packaging. TSMC shares rose about 2.3% in early Taipei trading Monday.

### Evidence hierarchy

- **Confirmed company information:** reporting date, quiet period, and published Q2 guidance.
- **Confirmed market information:** the Monday share move reported by Reuters.
- **Analyst interpretation:** Citi's target price, capacity estimates, and expectation of higher guidance.
- **Pending:** actual revenue, margins, node mix, packaging revenue, capex, utilization, and updated outlook.

The quiet period matters because management silence should not be read as evidence that rumours are correct. Until July 16, the correct benchmark is the official range.

India relevance: TSMC's demand mix affects Indian fabless teams through wafer pricing, PDK and IP schedules, packaging availability, tape-out queues, and customer budgets. Career relevance spans physical design, signoff, DFT, package co-design, supply planning, product management, and financial interpretation.

## 4. Policy Catch-Up: Who Gets Scarce Memory?

This section compares three positions rather than presenting a new regulation.

### Downstream industry position

A June 3 coalition of telecom, automotive, medical-device, retail, and broadband groups warned that AI data centers were consuming an outsized share of memory capacity. It asked the U.S. administration to support capacity expansion in the U.S. and allies, use trade agreements for resilience, ensure all market segments are served, ease redesign and alternative sourcing, and monitor supply-demand conditions.

### Political prioritization position

Senator Bernie Moreno's April letter argued that U.S. auto production should be fully supplied before memory is sold abroad. This prioritizes immediate domestic manufacturing continuity but could shift shortage costs to other countries and sectors.

### Supplier-industry position

Reporting on a July 1 SEMI letter says the association warned against interventions that distort pricing or capacity decisions, favouring U.S. investment and LTAs. A public primary copy of the SEMI letter was not located, so its wording is treated as reported rather than independently verified.

### Engineering and economics analysis

Memory is not perfectly interchangeable. Automotive DRAM, server DDR, mobile LPDDR, NAND, and HBM use different products, qualifications, packaging, and contracts. A government cannot instantly turn HBM output into qualified automotive memory. Practical policy tools therefore have different time horizons:

| Time horizon | Tool | Benefit | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| Immediate | Allocation or export prioritization | Protects selected factories | Transfers scarcity and may disrupt contracts or allies. |
| Near term | Redesign support, qualification acceleration, LTAs | Improves access and demand visibility | Qualification still takes time; buyers lose flexibility. |
| Long term | Fab incentives, tools, workforce, infrastructure | Expands resilient supply | High cost, execution delay, and future oversupply risk. |

No new export-control rule was verified today. These are proposals, letters, and reported lobbying positions, not enacted allocation policy.

## Concept Review

| Concept | Key distinction | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| ADS versus Korean share | The U.S. unit represents a defined fraction of the home-market share through a depositary. | Ten U.S. units equal one SK hynix common share. |
| Primary versus secondary sale | A primary issue gives cash to the company; a secondary sale pays the selling shareholder. | SK hynix is funding capex with new shares. |
| Financing versus capacity | Cash enables construction and tools; qualified output follows years later. | The offering does not relieve today's shortage. |
| Forecast versus actual | Analyst consensus is a benchmark; company results are evidence. | Samsung's 18-fold increase is not yet confirmed. |
| Guidance versus analyst target | Company guidance expresses management's range; a target price reflects an analyst model. | Citi's optimism is not TSMC guidance. |
| Allocation versus expansion | Allocation redistributes scarce supply; expansion increases future supply. | The policy tools solve different time horizons. |

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| <a id="daily-term-american-depositary-receipt-adr"></a>[**American Depositary Receipt (ADR)**](../knowledge-base/glossary.md#term-american-depositary-receipt-adr) | An ADS is the U.S.-traded ownership interest representing shares of a non-U.S. company held through a U.S. depositary bank; an ADR is the negotiable certificate evidencing that ADS interest. Market participants often use ADS and ADR interchangeably. |
| <a id="daily-term-analyst-consensus"></a>[**Analyst consensus**](../knowledge-base/glossary.md#term-analyst-consensus) | Analyst consensus aggregates independent forecasts into a market expectation against which the reported result is compared. It solves the need for a common benchmark, not the need for audited facts. |
| <a id="daily-term-average-selling-price-asp"></a>[**Average selling price (ASP)**](../knowledge-base/glossary.md#term-average-selling-price-asp) | ASP is product revenue divided by units or an industry-specific quantity such as bits shipped. It solves the pricing question when a company sells many grades, capacities, and contract terms. |
| <a id="daily-term-bookbuilding"></a>[**Bookbuilding**](../knowledge-base/glossary.md#term-bookbuilding) | Bookbuilding is the process in which underwriters collect indications of demand from prospective investors across prices and quantities, then use that order book to recommend the final offering price and allocation. It solves price discovery for a large new issue before public trading begins. |
| <a id="daily-term-capital-expenditure-capex"></a>[**Capital expenditure (capex)**](../knowledge-base/glossary.md#term-capital-expenditure-capex) | Capex is cash spent on long-lived productive assets such as land, buildings, cleanrooms, lithography systems, deposition and etch tools, packaging lines, and test equipment. It solves future capacity or technology needs, but spending is not current output. |
| <a id="daily-term-depositary-bank"></a>[**Depositary bank**](../knowledge-base/glossary.md#term-depositary-bank) | A depositary bank holds or arranges custody of the foreign company's underlying shares, issues the U.S. depositary securities, maintains holder records, processes dividends and voting instructions, and may charge custody or service fees. It solves the operational problem of connecting two securities markets and legal systems. |
| <a id="daily-term-depositary-ratio"></a>[**Depositary ratio**](../knowledge-base/glossary.md#term-depositary-ratio) | A depositary ratio states how many U.S. depositary shares represent one underlying foreign share, or vice versa. It solves practical pricing by making the U.S. unit trade at a familiar dollar amount even when the home-market share is very expensive. |
| <a id="daily-term-euv-lithography"></a>[**EUV lithography**](../knowledge-base/glossary.md#term-euv-lithography) | EUV lithography patterns a wafer using 13.5 nm light and reflective optics in vacuum. It solves the difficulty of printing the most intricate layers of advanced logic and DRAM with fewer multi-patterning steps than older deep-ultraviolet flows. |
| <a id="daily-term-financial-guidance"></a>[**Financial guidance**](../knowledge-base/glossary.md#term-financial-guidance) | Guidance is management's forward-looking range for revenue, margins, spending, or other business metrics based on current assumptions. It solves investor planning but remains uncertain until results are reported. |
| <a id="daily-term-form-f-1"></a>[**Form F-1**](../knowledge-base/glossary.md#term-form-f-1) | Form F-1 is the SEC registration statement commonly used by a foreign private issuer to offer securities publicly in the United States. It solves investor-disclosure requirements by providing extensive business, financial, risk, ownership, offering, and management information. |
| <a id="daily-term-form-f-6"></a>[**Form F-6**](../knowledge-base/glossary.md#term-form-f-6) | Form F-6 registers the depositary securities and their deposit agreement. It solves the legal and contractual setup for the U.S. receipt programme, including the depositary bank, fees, and rights attached to the receipts. |
| <a id="daily-term-high-bandwidth-memory-hbm"></a>[**High-Bandwidth Memory (HBM)**](../knowledge-base/glossary.md#term-high-bandwidth-memory-hbm) | HBM stacks multiple DRAM dies and connects them through dense vertical and package-level interconnects to provide a very wide, energy-efficient data path near an AI accelerator. It solves the memory-bandwidth bottleneck that leaves compute units idle while waiting for data. |
| <a id="daily-term-long-term-purchase-agreement-lta"></a>[**Long-term purchase agreement (LTA)**](../knowledge-base/glossary.md#term-long-term-purchase-agreement-lta) | An LTA commits a buyer and supplier to quantities, capacity reservations, pricing formulas, deposits, or purchase obligations over an extended period. It solves planning uncertainty in a capital-intensive market by giving the supplier demand visibility and the buyer access security. |
| <a id="daily-term-operating-profit"></a>[**Operating profit**](../knowledge-base/glossary.md#term-operating-profit) | Operating profit is revenue minus costs and expenses from core operations before financing costs and income taxes. It solves the question of how profitable the underlying business was without mixing in capital structure or tax effects. |
| <a id="daily-term-primary-offering"></a>[**Primary offering**](../knowledge-base/glossary.md#term-primary-offering) | A primary offering sells newly issued company shares so the issuer receives capital. It solves a funding need for factories, equipment, research, acquisitions, or debt reduction. |
| <a id="daily-term-quiet-period"></a>[**Quiet period**](../knowledge-base/glossary.md#term-quiet-period) | An earnings quiet period is a company-defined interval before results when management limits contact with investors and avoids selective discussion of material performance information. It solves fair-disclosure and information-asymmetry risk. |
| <a id="daily-term-shareholder-dilution"></a>[**Shareholder dilution**](../knowledge-base/glossary.md#term-shareholder-dilution) | Dilution is the reduction in an existing shareholder's proportional ownership and potentially earnings per share when a company issues additional shares. It solves no manufacturing problem by itself; it is the cost side of raising equity capital without repayment obligations. |
| <a id="daily-term-supply-allocation"></a>[**Supply allocation**](../knowledge-base/glossary.md#term-supply-allocation) | Supply allocation is the rule used to divide scarce output among customers, products, countries, or end uses. It solves an immediate shortage but creates opportunity costs because supplying one segment reduces availability elsewhere. |
